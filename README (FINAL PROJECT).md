For our final project, we are mainly just building on what we did with Assignment 4. We're expanding our 
monkey banana collecting game across our city, ocean and jungle scenes with a more cohesive story between them. 
We got some casual inspiration from games such as Goat Simulator, so we're adding health and hunger meters. 
Collecting bananas lowers hunger/health. We're also planning to add NPCs that react to the monkey passing by them, 
some road traffic in the city scene, animations for the monkey (walking, jumping, etc.), seamless transitions between 
scenes, and possibly some camera controls such as an adjustable FOV slider/camera angle options to make our 
scenes feel more alive.
